About our Waco clinic

One team, working together

At Anthony Medical, we believe true healing comes from addressing the root cause of pain, not just masking symptoms. Our integrated team of chiropractors, massage therapists, and nurse practitioners provide customized care plans to restore balance, improve function, and help you get back to doing what you love most.

Our providers use Diversified Adjusting, Thompson Drop-Table, Activator Methods, and Manual Adjusting, so the visit can match your comfort and what the exam shows.

Do you accept insurance?

Yes. We accept most major health insurance plans. Our team will verify your coverage before your visit, and that verification is free.

What should I expect during my first visit?

Your first visit is simple, welcoming, and informative. The team listens, evaluates your condition, and helps you take the first step. Many patients tell us they notice a change in how they move after that first session.
